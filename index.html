Portfolio

MOBY Robotics

MOBY builds autonomous deep-sea mining robots and AI-powered ocean technologies for defense, marine surveying, and sustainable critical mineral collection. A Forwards Fund angel invested in the friends and family round, February 2026.

Edgerun Industries

Edgerun (YC S26) builds military exoskeletons. Founder David Venegas previously co-founded Durin (autonomous mining robotics) and worked at Stoke Space and Hadrian. A Forwards Fund angel invested alongside Y Combinator in the pre-seed round, September 2026.

Manifesto

Critical Infrastructure

At Forwards Fund, we believe in the power of engineering and technology to solve humanity's greatest challenges. Our manifesto is a call to action for founders, builders, and visionaries who are unafraid to tackle the hardest problems in critical infrastructure. We champion first principles thinking, bold ambition, and a relentless drive to move the world forwards. Our mission is to empower pioneers who are building the foundations of tomorrow, today.

Deep Tech Renaissance

We invest in those who dare to reimagine the future of energy, mining, agriculture, manufacturing, logistics, and defense. We see a massive underrepresentation of deep tech startups in the American venture ecosystem, and we are committed to changing that. We seek to catalyze the next generation of transformative, meaningful, and high-impact companies that will define the 21st century and beyond.

Cowboy Capitalism

We embrace the vision of cowboy capitalism. A fearless, independent approach to innovation and entrepreneurship that fostered the American dream. Founders with contrarian perspectives, a chip on their shoulder, and seasoned personal convictions. Builders who value grit, resilience, and the rugged individualism that once built the American West. Visionaries with free-market thinking, frontier curiosity, a risk-tolerant mentality, and a pioneering spirit.

American Reindustrialization

We seek to rebuild the golden age of American manufacturing: onshore, made in the USA, and cutting-edge. We are unapologetically patriotic and seek to reindustrialize the nation that once saved the world from tyranny. We believe that a strong industrial base is essential for national security, economic prosperity, and the well-being of future generations.
